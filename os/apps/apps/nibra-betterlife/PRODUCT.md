# NIBRA BL BETTERLIFE
## Register
product
## Users
People balancing projects, conversations, and commitments at a desktop.
## Product Purpose
A consent-based local-first command center that clarifies today's priorities and prepares reviewable actions.
## Brand Personality
Calm, intelligent, human. Your private AI copilot for a better digital life.
## Anti-references
Generic purple SaaS, cluttered dashboards, robot graphics, hidden monitoring, fake connected services.
## Design Principles
Today comes first. Show evidence and inference. Keep access visible. Prepare before executing. Make memory editable.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard navigation, clear focus, accessible contrast, reduced motion, responsive narrow windows.
## Scene
A busy professional returns to their desktop between meetings in subdued office light; midnight surfaces reduce visual noise, warm ivory text and restrained orange make the next action clear. Refined light mode supports daylight work.
